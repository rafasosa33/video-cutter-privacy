Privacy Policy

VIDEO CUTTER 30-60

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VIDEO CUTTER 30-60 ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information and protect the privacy of our users. VIDEO CUTTER 30-60 is an Android application that allows users to trim and edit videos directly on their devices. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

2. Information We Collect

VIDEO CUTTER 30-60 does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its main features. We do not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or location data. We do not use analytics tools to track user behavior within the application.

3. Access to Photos and Videos

The App may request access to photos and videos stored on your device to allow you to:

  • Select videos from your gallery.
  • Trim and edit videos.
  • Adjust video duration.
  • Export and save edited videos.

Permissions depend on the Android version and the features being used. Users can manage or revoke permissions through their Android device settings.

4. Video Processing and Storage

VIDEO CUTTER 30-60 processes videos locally on the user's Android device. Selected and edited videos are not uploaded to our servers or transmitted to external servers for the App's main editing functions. We do not store copies of users' videos on our own servers. Video files remain under the user's control on their device, according to the available storage and export options.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

VIDEO CUTTER 30-60 may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. These providers may collect or use technical information, advertising identifiers, and information about ad interactions to display advertisements, measure their performance, and prevent fraudulent activity. The information collected depends on the advertising provider used and the user's device privacy settings. Third-party providers handle information according to their own privacy policies. Advertising does not change how the App processes videos locally.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent users' personal information. We do not share users' personal video files with third parties for processing. Advertising providers may receive technical information necessary to provide their advertising services, subject to their own privacy policies.

7. Security

We are committed to respecting and protecting our users' privacy. Local video processing means that video files do not need to be transmitted to our servers for the App's main editing functions. We recommend keeping your Android operating system updated.

8. Children's Privacy

VIDEO CUTTER 30-60 does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Third-party advertising services must be used in accordance with applicable laws and policies regarding children's privacy.

9. User Rights and Controls

Users can manage their privacy through the settings available on their Android devices. Users can:

  • Revoke access permissions to photos and videos.
  • Delete edited videos from their devices.
  • Manage available advertising preferences.
  • Stop using the App at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App's features, advertising services, or legal requirements. Any changes will be published on this page along with the updated revision date.
